Expository Notes

Classical mechanics: Although many mathematical techniques originated in the study of classical mechanics, their connection to mechanical systems are often neglected. The objective of these notes is to present the theory of classical mechanics in mathetmatical language, develop the resultant mathematical methods, and to see the physical systems as mathematical examples. This is an ongoing side project for personal instruction, and any comments are welcomed.
